Substash — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 10, 2026

The short version: your subscriptions never leave your device. Substash has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and never connects to your bank.

What Substash stores

The subscriptions you add — names, prices, billing cycles, renewal dates, and reminder preferences — are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. We have no servers and no way to see this data.

What Substash never collects

  • No account, email, or sign-up of any kind
  • No bank or card connections — Substash never asks for financial credentials
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers
  • No contacts, location, or browsing data
  • No access to your photo library — only the single screenshot you pick, read on your device and never stored or sent

The one thing that leaves your phone

To show what a subscription in another currency costs in yours, Substash downloads a public table of exchange rates. It is a plain request for a file that is the same for everyone: it carries none of your data, no identifier, and nothing about your subscriptions. Everything else — reading screenshots, reading statements, and every calculation — happens on your iPhone.

Purchases

The one-time lifetime unlock is processed by Apple. To validate the purchase, Substash uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and Apple's purchase receipt — nothing about your subscriptions, and nothing that identifies you personally.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Deleting your data

Use “Reset all data” in Settings, or simply delete the app — either removes everything, because everything lives on your device.

Children

Substash is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Given the app's design — no data collection — meaningful changes are unlikely.
