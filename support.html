Written by the person who made the app

Write to hi.pavelkorob@gmail.com. One person reads that inbox, and it is the same person who writes the code.

It did not find one of my subscriptions

That happens, and it is worth telling us about. Substash reads what is printed on the screen or in the statement, and banks print the same shop in a hundred different ways. Add the subscription by hand — Home, then the plus button — and if you can, write to us with the line as your bank printed it. Every line we get makes the reading better for everyone.

It shows a charge that is not a subscription

Nothing is added until you confirm it, so just leave it unchecked. If something you did not expect is suggested, tell us what it was: that is a mistake on our side, not yours.

My bank statement will not open

Some banks export a PDF that is a picture of the pages rather than text. Substash cannot read those. If your bank offers a different export — PDF instead of a scan — that one will work. A screenshot of your subscriptions list works too.

Does Substash cancel subscriptions for me?

No, and it never will. Cancelling is done in your Apple account or on the service's own site, and Substash takes you to the right page. We do not ask for your passwords and we cannot spend or move your money.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. There is nothing to connect. Substash reads a screenshot or a PDF file that you choose, on your iPhone, and that is the whole mechanism. Nothing is uploaded.

I paid and lost my purchase

The unlock is one payment, and it belongs to your Apple ID forever. Open Settings inside the app and tap “Restore purchases”. If it still does not come back, write to us with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, then “Reset all data” — or delete the app. Everything lives on your device, so either one removes all of it.
